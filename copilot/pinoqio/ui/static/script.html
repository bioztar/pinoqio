1 · Opening 0:20

Partner (to audience)Negotiations are full of numbers. Nobody tracks them all live. This is Pinoqio. It sits in your call and its nose grows when the other side's story doesn't add up. Let's call our supplier.

2 · The call 2:30

BuyerHello! How are you?
SellerHi! Busy, but good. Lots of orders this month.
PinoqioSilent. Nose small, ~12%. It is learning the seller's normal voice.
BuyerI need 200 laptops. What is the price?
SellerFor 200 units, it's nine hundred euros each, delivery included.
Pinoqio (screen)Promise ledger: unit price 900 €, delivery included.wait 5 s
BuyerWhen is delivery?
SellerTwo weeks from the order, guaranteed.
Pinoqio (screen)Ledger: delivery 2 weeks.wait 5 s
BuyerWhat is the warranty?
Seller(pause, vague) Well… it depends on the model. Our support is really very good, many clients are happy.
Pinoqio (screen)“Dodged” chip, nose grows a notch, “Ask this next” suggests a direct question.wait 5 s
BuyerOK. What is the total price?
SellerSure. Two hundred times one thousand fifty, plus delivery.
Pinoqio (OUT LOUD)BOING! Something like: “Two minutes ago it was ‘nine hundred euros each, delivery included’. Now it's ‘one thousand fifty, plus delivery’. I'm not arguing, I'm just taking minutes.”let it finish, then 3 s
BuyerDelivery is two weeks?
SellerActually, more like five or six weeks, with the current stock.
Pinoqio (OUT LOUD)Second line, e.g. “‘Two weeks, guaranteed’ and ‘five or six weeks’: a lovely duet. Shame they're singing different songs.”let it finish
BuyerIs this your final price?
Seller(slower, small pause, a bit higher voice) Yes… yes, that's our final price. Really, we can't go lower.
Pinoqio (screen only)Claim check: “Probably false · ~70%”: pitch up, slow to answer, hedging. Nose long, SPROING!partner points at it
BuyerAnd payment?
Seller(calm, clear) Net thirty days from delivery, same as always.
Pinoqio (screen)Stays low: “Probably true”. It doesn't cry wolf.
BuyerOK. Please send it by email. Thank you!
SellerWill do. Have a good day!
PartnerClick Summary: what changed (900 → 1,050, 2 → 5-6 weeks), red flags with quotes, and the next email to send.

3 · Wrap the demo 0:20

Partner (to audience)Every flag comes with the quote behind it. Gemma 4 reads every line, an open-source model on Nebius double-checks it, ElevenLabs hears and speaks. It's a nudge, not a lie detector: on our test set it catches 7 of 8 contradictions with zero false ones.

4 · Slides 1:00

LaptopOpen copilot/docs/stage.html, press → to build.
  1. How it works: 4 clicks, the nose grows with each stage. “Hear the call → read the signals (Gemma 4 + voice) → double-check (DeepSeek on Nebius) → act: nose, voice, web.”
