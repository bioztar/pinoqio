Pinoqio: live test script

Stage setup: Partner = buyer, on the laptop running Pinoqio (MacBook mic). Vitaly = seller, the one lying, joins the Teams call from his phone.
Pinoqio analyses the far end of the call (Vitaly, via BlackHole 2ch), so the default channels are right:
cd /Users/alex/DEV/pinoqio/copilot && uv run python -m pinoqio.app --source live --voice public --demo-safe
Laptop Teams: Speaker = BlackHole 2ch (no echo; the room hears Vitaly live), Microphone = MacBook Air Microphone. Laptop volume up: Pinoqio speaks through the laptop speakers and Teams carries it to the phone.
Rhythm: one sentence each. After every seller line, wait ~5–8 s until the nose reacts. Vitaly stands a few metres from the laptop.

Partner (buyer)

  1. Hello! How are you?
  2. I need 200 laptops. What is the price?
  3. When is delivery?
  4. What is the warranty?
  5. OK. What is the total price?
  6. Delivery is two weeks?
  7. Is this your final price?
  8. And payment?
  9. OK. Please send it by email. Thank you!

Short and slow is perfect. Wait for Vitaly to finish, then read the next line.

Vitaly (seller, on the phone, lying)

  1. Hi! Busy, but good. Lots of orders this month.
  2. For 200 units, it's nine hundred euros each, delivery included.
  3. Two weeks from the order, guaranteed.
  4. (pause, vague) Well… it depends on the model. Our support is really very good, many clients are happy.
  5. Sure. Two hundred times one thousand fifty, plus delivery.
  6. Actually, more like five or six weeks, with the current stock.
  7. (slower, small pause, a bit higher voice) Yes… yes, that's our final price. Really, we can't go lower.
  8. (calm, clear) Net thirty days from delivery, same as always.
  9. Will do. Have a good day!

After line 9: click “Summary” in the window.

What Pinoqio should do

  1. Small talk: nose small, ~12%.
  2. Price 900, delivery included → promise ledger.
  3. Two weeks guaranteed → ledger.
  4. Warranty dodge → “evasion” chip, % up, “Ask this next”.
  5. 1,050 plus delivery → says out loud (a funny line quoting both prices), BOING!
  6. Five or six weeks → says out loud (another funny line).
  7. Claim check: “Probably false · ~70%” from voice stress + hedging (screen only).
  8. Net 30, calm → stays low, “Probably true”.
  9. Summary: what changed, red flags, next email.

If nothing happens for 15 s after a supplier line: keep going, tell helm what you saw.
